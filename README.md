# Afric Dave Construction — plumbing website demo

Open `index.html` directly in your browser, or use your editor's Live Server extension. There are no dependencies or build steps.

All site files and downloaded stock photos are in this folder. The site uses Google Fonts when online and system-font fallbacks when offline.

## Customize

- Business name, copy, phone, email, address, credentials and reviews: `index.html`.
- Brand colors: the variables at the top of `styles.css`.
- Hero and service photography: `assets/`.
- Mobile navigation and form behavior: `script.js`.
